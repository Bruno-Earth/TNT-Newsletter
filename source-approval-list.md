# Proposed Source Approval List

Status: proposed for your review.

The Source & Data Agent should not treat these as fully approved until you confirm them. It can use them to prepare a first source audit and recommend the final trusted-source base.

## Tier 1: Official Market and Regulatory Sources

| Source | URL | Proposed Use | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ho Chi Minh Stock Exchange | https://www.hsx.vn/ | HOSE market data, VN-Index, VN30, listings, announcements | Official source; site may require JavaScript for some pages. |
| Hanoi Stock Exchange | https://www.hnx.vn/ | HNX, UPCoM, derivatives, bonds, announcements | Official source for HNX-operated markets. |
| State Securities Commission of Vietnam | https://ssc.gov.vn/ | Regulatory announcements and policy updates | Official regulator source; some pages require JavaScript. |
| State Bank of Vietnam | https://www.sbv.gov.vn/ | FX, interest rates, credit, banking policy, weekly banking activity | Official macro-financial source. |
| General Statistics Office | https://www.gso.gov.vn/en/homepage/ | CPI, GDP, macro releases | Official macro source. |
