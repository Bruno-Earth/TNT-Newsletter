# Daily Content Priority System

## Must Include Every Day

- VN-Index, HNX-Index, UPCoM movement
- Liquidity and total trading value
- Top gainers and losers, or biggest index contributors
- Foreign investor net buying and selling
- Major sector moves
- Key exchange, corporate, or regulatory announcements
- Short TNT Capital market view
- Vietnamese and English versions
- Source list
- Compliance checklist

## Include When Relevant

- Macro policy updates
- Interest rates, FX, inflation, credit growth
- Earnings updates
- Bond market and real estate credit news
- Derivatives and futures sentiment
- Global market context affecting Vietnam
- Commodity or currency moves that matter to Vietnamese sectors

## Optional If Useful

- Charts
- Sector rotation table
- Valuation snapshot
- Theme watchlist without stock recommendations
- What to monitor tomorrow

## Editorial Weighting

Use this rule when the day has too much information:

1. Market-wide facts before individual company news
2. Official sources before media interpretation
3. Liquidity and foreign flows before small headline noise
4. Policy and macro before rumor
5. Charts only when they clarify a point
